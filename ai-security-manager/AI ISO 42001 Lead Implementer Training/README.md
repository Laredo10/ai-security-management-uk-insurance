A professional study and implementation repository for organizing notes, templates, checklists, and working artifacts related to **ISO/IEC 42001 Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS)** implementation.

> **Important note:** This repository is intended for original study notes and implementation artifacts. It should not be used to reproduce or redistribute copyrighted course materials.

## What ISO/IEC 42001 Covers

ISO/IEC 42001 is a management system standard for artificial intelligence. It provides a structured framework for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an **Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS)**.

An effective AIMS helps organizations:

- align AI activities with business objectives
- define accountability and governance roles
- identify and treat AI-related risks
- apply appropriate AI controls
- manage documented information and operational processes
- monitor effectiveness and improve over time

## Core AIMS Concepts to Track in This Repository

Recommended terms and themes to standardize across your notes and artifacts:

- **AIMS**: Artificial Intelligence Management System
- **AI policy**: top-level direction and intent for AI governance
- **Scope**: organizational, technical, and physical boundaries of the AIMS
- **Interested parties**: stakeholders with relevant needs and expectations
- **Risk assessment**: process for identifying, analyzing, and evaluating AI-related risk
- **Impact assessment**: evaluation of potential effects on individuals, groups, society, and the organization
- **Statement of Applicability (SoA)**: record of selected controls and justification for inclusion or exclusion
- **Annex A controls**: reference controls supporting responsible AI management
- **Documented information**: controlled records and documents required for operation and evidence
- **Nonconformity**: failure to meet a stated requirement
- **Corrective action**: action taken to eliminate the cause of a nonconformity and prevent recurrence
- **Continual improvement**: ongoing enhancement of the effectiveness of the AIMS
