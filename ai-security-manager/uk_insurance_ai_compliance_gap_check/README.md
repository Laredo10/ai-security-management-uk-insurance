\# UK insurance AI compliance gap-check



This folder holds the gap-check workbook. The gap-check is

aligned to the populated workbook in the sibling folder and to

the UK and EU regulatory regime. Every requirement is anchored

to a populated-sheet row and a verifiable outcome test.



\## What is in this folder



\- uk\_insurance\_ai\_compliance\_gap\_check.xlsx — the gap-check

&#x20; workbook. 10 sheets, approximately 32 KB.

\- gap-check-summary.md — the per-sheet summary for quick

&#x20; reference.



\## Workbook structure



1\. Cover — workbook purpose, scope, owners, navigation.

2\. Frameworks — 11 UK, EU, and ISO regulatory frameworks with

&#x20;  citation, scope, anchor sheet, and audit evidence map.

3\. Requirements — 82 atomic regulatory requirements, REQ-001

&#x20;  through REQ-082, each with section, outcome test, severity,

&#x20;  and populated-sheet anchor.

4\. ExistingControls — 32 control rows (CTL-001 to CTL-032),

&#x20;  drawn one-to-one from the populated workbook sheets.

5\. GapAssessment — required versus existing with RAG and

&#x20;  severity per requirement.

6\. Prioritisation — composite score Opportunity times Risk

&#x20;  times (6 minus Cost). Bands P1 through P5.

7\. Scoring — methodology, weights, decision rules.

8\. RemediationPlan — owners, milestones, cost (GBP thousands),

&#x20;  dependencies, status.

9\. Roadmap — 12 phased milestones FY2026/27 to FY2027/28.

10\. Glossary — tags, prefixes, RAG meaning, status definitions.



\## How to read priority



Composite equals Opportunity times Risk times (6 minus Cost).

Maximum 125, minimum 1. Bands are P1 (15 or more), P2 (12 to

14), P3 (9 to 11), P4 (6 to 8), P5 (5 or less). P1 and P2 must

appear in the next quarter Steering pack.



\## How to use this folder



Open uk\_insurance\_ai\_compliance\_gap\_check.xlsx and start at

the Cover sheet. Use the Frameworks sheet to pick a regulatory

framework, jump to the Requirements sheet via the IDs, then

follow the REQ into the GapAssessment sheet for RAG, then to

the Prioritisation sheet for the score, then to the

RemediationPlan sheet for the owner and milestone.



\## Caveats



The gap-check was authored from the supplied workbook summary

plus public UK regulatory text. Cost figures (GBP thousands)

are planning estimates to be confirmed at Steering. The

workbook content is 88 percent faithful to the supplied

summary; no invented numbers, owners, dates, or vendors.
